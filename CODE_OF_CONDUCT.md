# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code, not people. Do not
harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or use sexualized
language or attention.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, reviews, discussions, and other
spaces where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

For sensitive conduct concerns, use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting channel and mark the report as a conduct concern. Do not publish
private evidence in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct is harmful to the community.
